Full-Day Jeep Tour + Overnight

The complete Wadi Rum in one day: red dunes, canyons and viewpoints, with time to walk, climb and take photos.

From40 JOD per personDurationFull day + 1 nightStarts9:00 AM

A full day in the jeep covering the main highlights of Wadi Rum. There is time to explore on foot, climb up for the views and take as many photos as you like, with lunch in the desert along the way.

In the evening we find a good spot to watch the sunset, then drive back to camp for dinner by the fire. Spend the night in a Bedouin tent, or sleep out under the stars.

What's included

  • Full-day jeep tour with a local guide
  • Lunch, dinner and breakfast
  • Overnight in a Bedouin tent
