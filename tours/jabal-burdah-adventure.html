Jabal Burdah Adventure + Overnight

Drive and hike to Jabal Burdah and climb up to its famous rock bridge, high above the desert floor.

From50 JOD per personDurationJeep + hike + 1 nightStarts9:00 AM

A jeep and hiking adventure to Jabal Burdah and its famous rock bridge, one of the most impressive natural arches in Wadi Rum. The climb involves steep sections and some scrambling, and the views from the top reach across the mountains.

After the hike, spend the night camping in the desert.

What's included

  • Jeep transfer and guided hike
  • Dinner and breakfast
  • Overnight in the desert

Fitness

Steep hiking with some scrambling. Tell us your experience and we'll advise.
