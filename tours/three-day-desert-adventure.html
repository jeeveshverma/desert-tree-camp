3-Day Wadi Rum Adventure

The hidden side of Wadi Rum: the famous sights by jeep, then Jabal Al-Hash, the White Desert and the remote valleys of Wadi Sabet.

From150 JOD per personDuration3 days + 2 nightsStartsPick-up in Wadi Rum Village

Experience the hidden side of Wadi Rum with local Bedouin guides, exploring mountains, white desert landscapes and remote valleys, and sleeping under the stars.

Choose two nights at the camp, or spend the second night in a natural desert cave. The cave is a simple, authentic desert experience: no electricity and no tent, just complete silence and the stars.

Day by day

Day 1: Full-day jeep tour of Wadi Rum

  • Lawrence Spring
  • Khazali Canyon
  • Little Bridge
  • Lawrence House
  • Sand dunes and sandboarding
  • Mushroom Rock
  • Umm Fruth Bridge
  • Abu Khashaba Canyon
  • Sunset in the desert with Bedouin tea
  • Dinner and overnight at the camp

Day 2: Jabal Al-Hash and the White Desert

  • Breakfast, then deep into the desert for the day
  • Jabal Al-Hash
  • The White Desert
  • Remote landscapes, hidden valleys and mountains
  • Bedouin lunch in the desert
  • Hiking and exploring
  • Sunset in the remote desert
  • Traditional Bedouin dinner and overnight at the camp

Optional: instead of returning to the camp, spend the night in a natural desert cave under the stars.

Day 3: Wadi Sabet and remote valleys

  • Breakfast, then on to the less-visited areas of Wadi Rum
  • Wadi Sabet
  • The Naghra and Al-Da'aa areas
  • Remote valleys and mountain formations
  • Short hikes and exploring
  • Bedouin lunch in the desert
  • Silence and landscapes away from the busy tourist areas
  • Return to Wadi Rum Village

Where you sleep

Option 1: Two nights at the camp

  • Night 1: private tent at the camp
  • Night 2: private tent at the camp

Option 2: One night in a desert cave

  • Night 1: private tent at the camp
  • Night 2: natural desert cave under the stars

What's included

  • 3 days of desert tours
  • 2 nights: camp, or camp + desert cave
  • Jeep transport during the tours
  • Bedouin guide
  • Breakfasts, lunches and dinners
  • Drinking water and Bedouin tea
  • Sandboarding
  • Village ↔ desert transport

Fitness

Short hikes on days 2 and 3. Fine for most people with normal fitness.
