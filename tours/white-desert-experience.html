White Desert Experience + Overnight

Leave the red sand behind for Wadi Rum's pale white desert, with its strange rock formations and quiet, open landscapes.

From50 JOD per personDurationDay tour + 1 nightStarts9:00 AM

Explore the white desert areas of Wadi Rum, where the sand turns pale and the rocks take on unusual shapes. It is a very different landscape from the classic red desert, and far quieter.

Finish the day with a night at camp, or sleep out under the stars.

What's included

  • Day tour to the white desert with a local guide
  • Dinner and breakfast
  • Overnight at camp or under the stars
